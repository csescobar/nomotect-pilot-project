# Privacy and LGPD

## Platform capabilities

- Data inventory support
- Consent records when applicable
- Data-subject export
- Correction workflows
- Anonymization and deletion requests
- Retention policies
- Processing records
- Audit trail for privacy operations

## Principles

- Data minimization
- Purpose limitation
- Least privilege
- Retention by policy
- Privacy by design
- Sensitive data redaction in logs and telemetry

## Important distinction

Technical capabilities support compliance but do not by themselves guarantee legal compliance. Each implementation must define its processing purposes, legal bases, retention periods and operational responsibilities.
